Your Naples golf coach, Joey Sandoval

Joey owns The Golf Club and leads its coaching. He comes from golf fitness and performance training, so he coaches the body that makes the swing, not just the club.

Every lesson works the same loop, and you leave with a plan instead of a list of tips.

  • Feel it

    RoboGolfPro guides you through takeaway, transition and impact until the motion is yours.

  • See it

    Sportsbox AI and video show what your hips, shoulders and spine are doing, compared to tour benchmarks.

  • Measure it

    TrackMan reads club and ball speed, launch, spin, path and carry, so you know the change is real.

Lessons with Ryan Hogan

Ryan coaches for the course. He takes what the robot, TrackMan and the 3D analysis show you and boils it down to a few things you can practice and play with.

  • Lesson plus practice15 to 20 minutes of instruction, then 30 to 40 minutes of guided practice, mobility or routine work.
  • Practice planningA practice plan and program built around your game, so range time carries to the course.
  • Mental performance coachingInstruction for your routines, focus and decisions over the ball.
  • Speed training classGroup class for adding clubhead speed. Coming soon.
  • Warm-up clinicA personalized warm-up built for injury prevention and better play from the first tee. Coming soon.
